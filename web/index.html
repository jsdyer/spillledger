Sources & method

The 2023–24 federal fund report lists incident reports and claims. This edition covers the general-process table for BC and Nova Scotia. An incident can have several claimants. An offer, a claim and money recovered are different stages. A dash means the fund published no entry in that cell. Preventive responses can appear under “No spill.” Status is as printed in that report.
